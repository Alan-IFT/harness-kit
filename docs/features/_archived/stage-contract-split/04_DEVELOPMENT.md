## Insight to surface

- 2026-08-01 · A naming ban stated by counter-example defeats its own verification: writing "`04_DEVELOPMENT.md`, not `04_IMPLEMENTATION.md`" into a contract, a release note or a report template is the natural way to bind the rule and it makes the repo-wide sweep that checks the rule return the banned token, so the ban must be phrased by class ("never coin a synonym or a partition-suffixed variant") and the frozen-heading example must carry the frozen bytes · evidence: `agents/solution-architect.md:61`, `agents/pm-orchestrator.md:39`, `CHANGELOG.md:15` and `agents/qa-tester.md:108`, all four rewritten after the sweep flagged them
- 2026-08-01 · Amending a normative rule silently falsifies its paraphrases, and the paraphrases are the surfaces readers actually load: this round's one-clause change to boundary-rule row 2 falsified a sentence in the rule's own closing paragraph, a glossary entry in `CONTEXT.md` and a trigger enumeration in the shipped release note, none of which any check reads — so a rule amendment must ship with a sweep for every restatement of the clause it changes, not just an edit to the clause · evidence: `.harness/rules/70-doc-size.md:101-103`, `CONTEXT.md:62-64` and `CHANGELOG.md:14`, all three corrected after gate C-13 named only the first two
- 2026-08-01 · A contract that inherits a document schema **by reference** silently drops every instruction that the inline version renders *inside* the schema example rather than as a numbered rule: the three inline partition templates carry the `> Contract portion.` header as a line of their fenced `04_DEVELOPMENT.md` skeleton, so "same schema as `dev-frontend.md`" reads as covering it, and two of the three by-reference templates therefore shipped with no header instruction at all while the third spelled it out — inheritance by reference must enumerate the schema's out-of-band instructions, because nothing in the referring text distinguishes an instruction that travels from one that does not · evidence: `backend/dev-db.md.tmpl:76` (header inside the fenced example) vs `backend/dev-api.md.tmpl:65` (spelled out) vs `backend/dev-services.md.tmpl` and `fullstack/dev-backend.md.tmpl` (absent until QA-12)
- 2026-08-01 · Adding a required heading to an authoring template fixes a heading-presence audit without moving its line-count audit: E29 put `## Summary` and `## Verdict` into the stage-7 delivery template, which stops AP-2's missing-heading WARN that fired on 40 of 40 archived deliveries, yet the minimum conforming delivery is still exactly 15 lines against a 15-line threshold — the two clauses of one check fail independently and fixing one leaves the other at margin 0 · evidence: `agents/pm-orchestrator.md:199-232` vs `agents/supervisor.md:101`, measured by constructing the minimum document
